# Transfer Types

Cross-world transfer is not a single phenomenon. Multiple distinct transfer types may exist, each with different properties, consequences, and metaphysical implications.

## Candidate Transfer Types

The architecture must accommodate:

### Full Physical Transfer

The entire physical body crosses from one world to another. The agent arrives with:

- Biological structure intact
- Physical injuries or conditions preserved
- Clothing and carried objects (depending on transfer rules)
- Immediate physical needs (hunger, thirst, temperature regulation)

### Identity-Pattern Transfer

A pattern representing the agent's identity crosses worlds and binds to local biological or constructed substrate. Properties:

- The original body remains in the source world (or is destroyed)
- A new body is constructed or appropriated in the target world
- Memories, personality, and skills may persist
- Physical capabilities may differ based on the new substrate

### Partial Memory Transfer

Only some information crosses. The agent in the target world:

- May have fragmentary memories of the source world
- May have skills without declarative knowledge of how they acquired them
- May have emotional associations without remembered causes
- May have dreams or visions rather than clear memories

### Reincarnation-like Binding

An identity pattern binds to a newly developing biological entity (fetus, infant, hatchling):

- The foreign pattern coexists with local biological development
- The resulting agent has hybrid properties
- Foreign memories may emerge gradually during development
- The local culture shapes how the foreign component is interpreted

### Informational Echo

A pattern of information crosses without any associated identity:

- A concept, technique, or story appears in the target world
- No agent arrives to explain it
- The information may be attributed to divine revelation, dream, or madness
- It may spread as a meme without any physical carrier

### Artifact Transfer

A physical object crosses without a living agent:

- The object has source-world properties
- It may interact unexpectedly with target-world physics or mana
- It may be interpreted as a gift, a threat, or a natural phenomenon
- It may become a focus of religious, scientific, or political attention

### Overlapping Identity Patterns

Multiple identity patterns from different sources bind to the same target entity:

- The resulting agent has conflicting memories, skills, or loyalties
- The overlap may be stable or unstable
- It may produce unusual cognitive or magical properties

## Phase 22 Representation

Candidate mechanisms are not encoded as `TransferType` variants. `TransferMechanismSchemaId` identifies an opaque versioned adapter contract, while objective payload fingerprints and explicit property correspondences record what an adapter claims persisted or transformed. A committed receipt must cover every payload and continue the exact declared causes.

## Metaphysical Openness

Phase 22 deliberately does not select a final metaphysical model. These transfer types remain candidate interpretations and research scenarios, not authoritative semantic variants.
