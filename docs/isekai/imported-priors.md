# Imported Priors

Imported priors are the conceptual frameworks, beliefs, and expectations that transferred agents bring from their origin world. They are not technologies. They are ways of thinking that may dramatically alter causal inference and search behavior in Causafera.

## Knowledge Is Not Technology

The Phase 22 contract enforces this structurally. Imported priors are subjective patterns. Reproduction requirements separately name practices, materials, resource schemas, and measurement schemas; only independently supplied `CapabilityEvidence` can close those gaps. A transfer trace alone grants no capability.

Earth knowledge must not behave as a technology unlock. An isekai arrival may know:

> Microorganisms can cause disease.

That does not automatically provide:

- Microscopes
- Glass purity
- Sterile tools
- Experimental infrastructure
- Scientific credibility

Technology requires:

```text
concept
+
materials
+
tools
+
measurement
+
procedural knowledge
+
social transmission
```

## What Priors Actually Do

An isekai arrival primarily introduces:

- **Foreign priors** - Beliefs about how the world works that may be true, false, or locally inapplicable
- **Search directions** - Hunches about where to look for solutions
- **Conceptual frameworks** - Ways of organizing observations that may or may not fit local phenomena
- **Expectations** - What counts as normal, surprising, or impossible

These can dramatically alter causal inference without immediately producing technology.

## Examples of Imported Prior Effects

### Germ Theory

An arrival with germ theory may:

- Look for invisible causes of disease
- Advocate for cleanliness without knowing which practices matter
- Inspire local researchers to investigate microscopic phenomena
- Be dismissed as insane by authorities

### Physics

An arrival with Newtonian physics may:

- Expect consistent gravitational behavior
- Be surprised by mana-mediated forces
- Attempt to apply conservation laws that do not hold
- Develop a hybrid physics that is locally useful but globally inconsistent

### Social Concepts

An arrival with democratic ideals may:

- Advocate for institutions that do not fit local power structures
- Be co-opted by existing factions
- Inspire genuine reform or violent reaction
- Be completely ignored

## Prior Contamination

Imported priors spread through local populations via:

- Direct communication with the arrival
- Documents produced by or about the arrival
- Institutions founded by the arrival or their followers
- Cultural practices adopted from the arrival's example

This spread is not necessarily beneficial. A false prior may be more compelling than a true one, especially if it fits existing local beliefs or serves powerful interests.
