# Translation Impact

When isekai arrivals attempt to communicate foreign concepts in local languages, they create translation effects that may reshape local conceptual systems, technological directions, and magical practices.

## The Translation Problem

A foreign concept may not map cleanly to local concepts. The arrival must approximate:

```text
source communicative structure
↓
source concepts
↓
translator concept mappings
↓
target conceptual approximations
↓
target language encoding
```

The arrival is both source and translator. Their local language competence may be imperfect. Their understanding of local concepts may be limited. The result is often creative, approximate, and misleading.

## Concept Gaps

Some foreign concepts have no local equivalent:

```text
Earth concept: "computer"
Local approximations:
    - thinking mechanism
    - calculation device
    - memory machine
    - thinking loom
```

Different arrivals may produce different approximations. Different local listeners may understand different things from the same approximation.

## Translation Effects

Translations may create:

- **Loanwords** - Direct adoption of foreign phonological forms
- **New concepts** - Local speakers developing concepts they previously lacked
- **Semantic distortions** - Foreign concepts mapped to partially incompatible local concepts
- **New technological directions** - Mistranslated concepts inspiring novel approaches

## Example: The "Thinking Loom"

An arrival attempts to explain computers:

```text
Arrival: "A device that performs calculations automatically"
Local weaver: "Like a loom that weaves numbers instead of thread?"
Arrival: "Yes, exactly like that"
```

Result: Local researchers develop mechanical calculation devices using loom-like architectures, producing a completely different technological path from Earth computing.

## Translation and Magic

If a translated concept becomes embedded in ritual or magical practice, translation errors may have magical consequences:

- A mistranslated ritual instruction produces a different physical pattern
- The different pattern couples with mana differently
- The result is a new magical effect, possibly unintended
