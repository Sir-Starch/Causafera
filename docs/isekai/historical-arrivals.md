# Historical Arrivals

Cross-world transfer is not a one-time event. It has a history within Causafera, with arrivals at different times, places, and scales, each with different consequences.

## Arrival as Historical Event

Phase 22 provides a causal transfer record suitable for a future historical-bootstrap adapter: scheduled/completed times, target place, opaque mechanism identity, canonical payload results, and committed ancestry. It does not generate example arrivals or classify their long-term significance.

Each arrival is a historical event with:

- A time and place
- A transfer type
- An arriving entity or pattern
- Immediate physical consequences
- Social reactions
- Long-term causal effects

## Patterns of Arrival

Historical arrival patterns may include:

- **Sporadic individuals** - Single arrivals separated by long intervals
- **Clustered arrivals** - Multiple arrivals in a short time or place
- **Institutionalized arrivals** - Regular, predictable transfer events
- **Artifact-only arrivals** - Objects without living agents
- **Informational echoes** - Concepts appearing without physical carriers

## Consequences of Arrival History

The history of arrivals shapes Causafera:

- Languages may contain loanwords from multiple source worlds
- Technologies may show hybrid influences from different origins
- Social institutions may have been founded by or in reaction to arrivals
- Religious doctrines may incorporate arrival narratives
- Magical practices may have been altered by foreign concepts

## Arrival and Local Response

Local societies may respond to arrivals in various ways:

- **Integration** - Absorbing the arrival into existing social structures
- **Exclusion** - Marginalizing or expelling the arrival
- **Exploitation** - Using the arrival's knowledge for local benefit
- **Veneration** - Treating the arrival as divine or prophetic
- **Extermination** - Killing the arrival as a threat
- **Institutionalization** - Creating permanent structures to manage arrivals

These responses have long-term consequences for how subsequent arrivals are treated.

## Documenting Arrivals

The observer layer may track arrival history:

```text
Arrival Event 47
Time: Year 312, Month 4, Day 17
Location: North Gate district
Transfer type: Full physical transfer
Origin: Unknown (possibly Earth-like world)
Immediate consequence: Agent appeared in marketplace, caused panic
Long-term effects: Founded School of Applied Mathematics (Year 318)
                     Introduced concept of "zero" (Year 320)
                     Executed for heresy (Year 327)
                     Mathematical tradition continued by students
```
