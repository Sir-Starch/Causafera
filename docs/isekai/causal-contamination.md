# Causal Contamination

Causal contamination is the process by which foreign concepts, practices, and patterns introduced by isekai arrivals alter local causal chains in Causafera. It is not merely cultural influence. It is a physical process by which imported information changes behaviour, which changes physical structures, which may alter mana responses.

## The Contamination Chain

```text
isekai arrival
↓
foreign concept or practice introduced
↓
local agent adopts or adapts it
↓
behaviour changes
↓
repeated physical or informational structure
↓
local mana response
↓
changed ground truth
```

## Types of Contamination

### Conceptual Contamination

Foreign concepts alter how local agents categorize and reason about the world:

- A new disease concept leads to different treatment practices
- A new physical theory leads to different experimental designs
- A new social concept leads to different institutional structures

### Practical Contamination

Foreign practices alter local behaviour patterns:

- A new farming technique changes land use patterns
- A new military tactic changes conflict dynamics
- A new craft method changes material flows

### Linguistic Contamination

Foreign language elements alter local linguistic systems:

- Loanwords introduce new phonological patterns
- New concepts require new lexical distinctions
- Translation errors create new semantic associations

### Magical Contamination

Foreign patterns may have unexpected mana interactions:

- A foreign ritual pattern couples with local mana differently
- A foreign symbol creates a new mana attractor
- A foreign practice creates a new stable physical pattern

## Contamination May Be Beneficial or Harmful

Causal contamination is value-neutral:

- A foreign agricultural practice may increase yields
- A foreign disease concept may lead to effective prevention
- A foreign political idea may inspire beneficial reform

Or:

- A foreign agricultural practice may deplete soil
- A foreign disease concept may lead to harmful treatments
- A foreign political idea may provoke destructive conflict

## Contamination Is Irreversible

Once a foreign concept or practice has entered local causal chains, it cannot be cleanly removed:

- Documents have been copied and distributed
- Practices have been taught and habituated
- Concepts have been integrated into local reasoning
- Physical structures have been built
- Mana patterns have been established

Even if all arrivals are expelled and all foreign documents burned, the causal effects persist in altered behaviour, institutions, and physical environment.
