# Practice Lineages

A practice lineage records structural ancestry, not a human classification. Each child cites one parent and owns a complete bounded validated program. The changed instruction is visible by comparing parent and child.

Lineages may diverge even when agents describe them as the same practice, and unrelated lineages may converge structurally. Observer glosses such as “ritual” or “maintenance routine” are downstream classifications and cannot feed back into simulation state.

Future transmission models may create children through observed demonstrations, instruction, documents, error, or deliberate experimentation. They must retain the perceptual or informational support for the change and use deterministic scheduler-provided randomness where variation is stochastic.
