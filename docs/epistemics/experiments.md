# Experiments

Experiments are structured interventions designed to test hypotheses about causal relationships. They are a central mechanism of knowledge progress, but they are socially situated, resource-intensive, and fallible.

## Experiment Structure

An experiment involves:

- **Hypothesis** - A proposed causal relationship to test
- **Design** - A plan for creating conditions that distinguish the hypothesis from alternatives
- **Intervention** - A deliberate manipulation of some variable
- **Observation** - Measurement of outcomes
- **Comparison** - Contrast between intervention and non-intervention conditions
- **Inference** - Drawing conclusions from the comparison

## Experiment Types

The architecture must support:

- **Controlled trials** - Comparing intervention against a non-intervention control
- **Natural experiments** - Exploiting naturally occurring variation
- **Systematic observation** - Structured recording without intervention
- **Replication** - Repeating an earlier experiment to verify results

## Experiment and Error

Experiments fail in predictable ways:

- **Confounding** - An unmeasured variable affects both intervention and outcome
- **Selection bias** - The groups compared differ in ways other than the intervention
- **Measurement error** - Imprecise or inaccurate observation
- **Publication bias** - Only successful experiments are reported
- **Fraud** - Deliberate data fabrication

## Experiment and Society

Experiments require social support:

- Resources (materials, instruments, time)
- Skills (design, execution, analysis)
- Institutions (funding, peer review, publication)
- Credibility (why should others believe the results?)

A society without experimental institutions may have brilliant individual observers but no systematic knowledge progress.

## Experiment and Magic

In a mana-sensitive world, experiments may have unintended magical consequences:

- Repeated standardized interventions create temporal patterns
- Laboratory spaces accumulate mana from repeated similar practices
- Measurement rituals become magically relevant
