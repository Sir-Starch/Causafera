# Language Change

Language change is the cumulative effect of many local processes operating on phonology, morphology, lexicon, and grammar. It is not a single mechanism but an emergent property of communication within communities.

## Types of Language Change

The architecture must support future implementation of:

- **Sound change** - Systematic phonetic shifts affecting classes of sounds
- **Phonetic drift** - Gradual articulatory shifts without clear conditioning
- **Lexical replacement** - Old forms replaced by new ones for the same concepts
- **Borrowing** - Forms adopted from contact languages
- **Grammaticalization** - Lexical items becoming grammatical morphemes
- **Construction reanalysis** - Listeners parsing structures differently from speakers
- **Semantic change** - Shifts in the concepts associated with forms

## Example: Grammaticalization Pathway

```text
frequent lexical sequence
↓
phonological reduction
↓
boundary weakening
↓
reanalysis
↓
new productive morpheme
```

A phrase used together so often that it fuses into a single unit, then gets reinterpreted as a morphological marker.

## Resolution of Language Change

Language evolution operates statistically at language-community or cohort resolution. Do not perform high-resolution acoustic simulation of every ordinary conversation.

Causal Resolution may increase linguistic detail for:

- Magically important chants
- Historically important terms
- Language-contact zones
- Semantic innovations

## Language Change and Magic

Language is physically relevant because utterances create acoustic and temporal patterns. Mana does not understand words, but it may respond to:

- Sound frequency
- Timing
- Repetition
- Synchronization
- Stable sequences

Therefore language change may alter magical practices:

```text
historical vowel shift
↓
changed acoustic signature
↓
changed mana coupling
↓
gradual spell instability
```

Agents may interpret this as declining discipline, divine punishment, or corrupted teaching. The actual physical cause may be language change.
