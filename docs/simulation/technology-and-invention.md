# Technology and Invention

Causafera has no predefined technology tree. Technical capabilities emerge from the intersection of multiple independent requirements.

## Requirements for New Technology

A new technical capability may require:

- conceptual knowledge
- material availability
- tools
- precision
- measurement
- procedural knowledge
- motor skill
- social transmission

A person may understand a principle but be unable to reproduce it. Knowledge alone is never sufficient.

## Knowledge Types

Separate:

- declarative knowledge (knowing that)
- procedural knowledge (knowing how)
- perceptual expertise (recognizing patterns)
- motor skill (physical execution)

An isekai arrival may know that microorganisms cause disease. That does not automatically provide microscopes, glass purity, sterile tools, experimental infrastructure, or scientific credibility.

## Technology Dynamics

Technological knowledge can be:

- **lost** - when practitioners die without transmission;
- **locally isolated** - known in one region but not another;
- **copied incorrectly** - errors in transmission produce different variants;
- **institutionally restricted** - controlled by organizations for strategic reasons.

## Emergence Path

Preferred historical process for familiar technical categories:

```text
social need
→ conceptual hypothesis
→ material experimentation
→ stable procedure
→ tool development
→ measurement system
→ institutional standardization
→ widespread adoption
→ local adaptation
```
