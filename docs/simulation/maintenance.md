# Maintenance

Infrastructure and equipment degrade. Maintenance is not a decorative detail. It is a causal process that shapes historical outcomes.

## Degradation and Repair

Plan support for:

- wear - gradual performance reduction through use;
- repair - restoring function without full replacement;
- maintenance practices - scheduled or condition-based upkeep;
- specialist knowledge - skills required for effective maintenance;
- replacement components - supply chains for parts and materials.

## Historical Consequences

Historical crises may result from loss of maintenance knowledge.

**Example causal chain:**

```text
specialist dies
↓
maintenance practice lost
↓
pump repairs degrade
↓
water system failures accumulate
↓
district health changes
```

No `WaterCrisisEvent` is required. The crisis emerges from lower-level causal processes.

## Maintenance and Magic

In an information-sensitive magical world, maintenance practices may create persistent patterns:

- repeated inspection routes;
- standardized repair sequences;
- tool movements with stable geometry;
- rhythmic work patterns.

These may couple with local mana fields and produce emergent effects unrelated to the original technical purpose.
