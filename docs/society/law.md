# Law

Law exists through texts, interpretations, precedent, authority, and enforcement. It is not a primitive engine system. It is an emergent social institution built from documents, practices, and power relationships.

## Law as Information Structure

A law is not `active: bool`. It is:

- a text or oral tradition;
- an interpretation by authorities;
- a precedent from past cases;
- an enforcement mechanism;
- a social understanding that may vary between groups.

## Legal Evolution

Laws develop historically:

- customary practices become codified;
- precedents accumulate interpretive weight;
- authorities issue rulings that constrain future cases;
- social changes create pressure for legal adaptation;
- conflicts between jurisdictions produce complexity.

## Law and Magic

In an information-sensitive magical world, legal practices may unintentionally create mana structures:

- repeated oaths with stable wording;
- standardized contract forms;
- seals and signatures used consistently;
- court procedures with rhythmic or geometric patterns;
- legal documents stored in specific arrangements.
