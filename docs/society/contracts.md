# Contracts

Contracts are agreements that create mutual obligations. They are not primitive `ContractMagic` objects. They are socially constructed information structures that may, through long historical processes, acquire magical properties.

## Implemented foundation

Phase 19 stores an `AttestedAgreement` as a traced physical document association with opaque parties, witnesses, supporting authority, and formation time. This records attestation, not shared meaning, performance, legal validity, enforcement, or magical effect.

## Contract Structure

A contract may involve:

- parties with defined roles;
- terms and conditions;
- performance requirements;
- penalties or remedies;
- witnesses or guarantors;
- temporal scope;
- modification and termination clauses.

## Contract Enforcement

Enforcement mechanisms may include:

- social reputation and trust networks;
- third-party arbitration;
- institutional authority;
- physical collateral or hostages;
- magical consequences (if emergent from practice history).

## Magical Contracts

Magical contracts should be a possible emergent result of long-lived contract practices. They are not a primitive engine feature.

**Candidate emergence path:**

```text
repeated contract form
→ stable textual pattern
→ repeated ritual execution
→ mana coupling
→ persistent enforcement effect
→ perceived as magical contract
```
