# Strategic Communication

Communication is not assumed honest. Agents may use communication strategically to achieve goals, including deception.

## Communication Components

Separate:

```text
belief
communicative intent
utterance
listener interpretation
```

These components may diverge. An agent may utter something they do not believe, or a listener may interpret something differently from the speaker's intent.

## Communicative Strategies

An agent may:

- **Report honestly**: state belief accurately
- **Exaggerate**: state belief more strongly than held
- **Conceal**: omit relevant information
- **Fabricate**: state something known to be false
- **Signal group membership**: use in-group language or references
- **Repeat without belief**: relay information without endorsing it
- **Strategic ambiguity**: be vague to avoid commitment
- **Framing**: present information to bias interpretation

## Deception

Deception is a major target causal phenomenon:

- **False claim**: statement contradicting speaker's belief
- **Half-truth**: true statement with misleading implication
- **Omission**: relevant information left unstated
- **Distraction**: diverting attention from relevant information

## Deception Detection

Listeners may detect deception through:

- **Inconsistency**: contradiction with known facts
- **Behavioral cues**: unusual behavior (unreliable)
- **Source reputation**: known dishonesty of source
- **Plausibility check**: does claim make causal sense
- **Independent verification**: checking against other sources

## Strategic Communication and Magic

In an information-sensitive magical world, strategic communication has physical consequences:

- **Repeated false claim**: may create repeated behavior
- **Synchronized false belief**: may create coordinated action
- **Persistent structure**: long-held false beliefs create stable patterns
- **Mana response**: these patterns may affect mana fields

A strategic lie may eventually become physically reinforced through the behavior it creates.

This is a major target causal phenomenon.

## Communication and Trust

Strategic communication affects trust:

- **Honesty builds trust**: truthful communication increases trust
- **Deception destroys trust**: detected deception decreases trust
- **Reputation effects**: deception reputation spreads socially
- **Cynicism**: repeated deception creates general distrust

## Communication and Society

Strategic communication shapes society:

- **Propaganda**: organized strategic communication
- **Rumor**: unverified information spreading socially
- **Gossip**: social information exchange with strategic intent
- **Negotiation**: strategic communication to reach agreement
- **Ritual**: communication with symbolic and strategic functions

## Determinism

Strategic communication must be deterministic given:

- speaker's belief state
- speaker's goal state
- speaker's model of listener
- social context
- trust relationships
- personality parameters

## Performance

Strategic communication may be complex. Strategies:

- Simplified models for routine communication
- Detailed models for significant interactions
- Event-driven processing for deception detection
