# What Makes Ontopolis Unique

## Causal Reconstructability

A surprising phenomenon must be reconstructable as a causal history.

## No Semantic Shortcuts

The engine does not contain primitive `Class`, `Skill`, `Level`, or `Monster` systems.

These must emerge from lower-level causal processes.

## Language Is Physical

Language change can alter magical practices because utterances create acoustic and temporal patterns.

Mana does not understand words.

## Observation Is Not Ground Truth

Agents construct subjective models from incomplete sensory access.

Different agents may construct incompatible concepts from the same population.

## Geography Is Causal

Geography is not decorative. It modifies:

- material availability
- pathogen ecology
- trade routes
- mana topology
- language contact
- political boundaries

## Biology Is Causal

Biological structure and variation create:

- perceptual differences
- developmental differences
- health patterns
- demographic consequences
- social categories

Social categories need not match biological structure.
