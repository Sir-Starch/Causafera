# Core Causal Loop

The central loop of Ontopolis is:

```text
GROUND TRUTH
    ↓
PHYSICAL ACCESS
    ↓
SENSORY ACQUISITION
    ↓
GENERIC FEATURE EXTRACTION
    ↓
PERCEPTION
    ↓
SUBJECTIVE CONCEPT FORMATION
    ↓
SUBJECTIVE CAUSAL INFERENCE
    ↓
BELIEF / HYPOTHESIS
    ↓
DECISION
    ↓
BEHAVIOUR / PRACTICE
    ↓
REPEATED PHYSICAL OR INFORMATIONAL STRUCTURE
    ↓
LOCAL MANA RESPONSE
    ↓
CHANGED GROUND TRUTH
    ↓
NEW OBSERVATION
```

## Social Transmission

Language and social transmission connect subjective states between agents:

```text
AGENT CONCEPT
    ↓
COMMUNICATIVE INTENT
    ↓
LANGUAGE ENCODING
    ↓
PHYSICAL UTTERANCE / DOCUMENT
    ↓
LANGUAGE DECODING
    ↓
LISTENER CONCEPT MAPPING
```

The recipient does not directly receive the speaker's concept.

Meaning must be reconstructed.
