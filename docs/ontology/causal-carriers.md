# Causal Carriers

Causal carriers are the mechanisms by which state from one domain affects another.

## Physical Carriers

- Position and motion
- Material contact
- Energy transfer
- Field interaction
- Structural connection

## Informational Carriers

- Physical utterances
- Written documents
- Imitated practices
- Institutional records
- Reputational transmission

## Biological Carriers

- Pathogen transmission
- Genetic inheritance
- Symbiotic transfer
- Ecological pressure

## Social Carriers

- Role assignment
- Rule enforcement
- Resource allocation
- Ritual synchronization
- Spatial organization

## Implemented carrier boundaries

Phase 6 implements an append-only causal event graph in `ontopolis-core`. Objective property transitions retain ordered prior `TraceId` causes and before/after canonical fingerprints. Opaque numeric schema IDs replace semantic event strings.

Phase 7 implements the first physical-access carrier boundary in `ontopolis-perception`. Property-based signals pass through channel, time, integer range, and magnitude-threshold constraints before becoming relative sensory samples. Samples retain supporting traces.

Phase 8 generic extraction accepts only acquired samples and retains flattened causal input spans for every emitted magnitude/change feature. Authoritative source identity remains extractor bookkeeping. The Phase 9 bridge strips it and produces `PerceptualCue` with only agent-local subjective evidence and quantized properties; cognition never receives the causal trace correspondence.

Phases 11–12 carry subjective learning ancestry through `PerceptId` and opaque agent-local identifiers. Concepts retain the percept that last activated or revised a prototype. Beliefs retain bounded `EvidenceId`, `SubjectiveSourceId`, and percept support; causal hypotheses relate only `SubjectivePatternId` values. None of these IDs reveal authoritative entities, places, bodies, feature IDs, or Ground Truth traces.

Phases 13–14 implement language as an informational carrier boundary. Speaker-private intent is encoded into an ordered physical form; decoding consumes the observed form and listener-local context, never the intent. Lexeme ancestry, bounded use history, percept-supported transmission, lexical pressure, and subjective semantic updates preserve why a form exists and how an interpretation was learned without making meaning objective.

Phases 15–16 implement imitated programs, measurements, calibrations, and documents as bounded informational carriers. Practice execution emits only timed action proposals from subjective evidence. Measurements transform accessible observations through opaque unit/calibration conventions and retain uncertainty. Documents store physical glyph IDs and explicit copy edits with ancestry; neither practices nor glyphs contain authoritative English meaning.

Phase 17 implements local mana field interaction as a physical carrier. Trace-backed physical samples are grouped only by canonical structural fingerprint; recurrence, interval regularity, synchronization, repeated coordinates, magnitude, diffusion, decay, and saturation determine replacement-state proposals. Changed cells preserve sample and neighbouring-field causes, and a new committed trace is required for every accepted cell transition. No belief, concept, word, practice meaning, or observer classification crosses this boundary.

Phase 18 implements directed causal relevance as a scheduling-detail carrier. Domain adapters emit numeric, trace-backed signals on registered opaque channel IDs; the resolution reducer never sees trade, migration, political, religious, geographic, or observer labels. Canonical fixed-point weighting, decay, saturation, thresholds, and hysteresis produce replacement proposals. Every changed chunk entry retains direct signal and prior-state causes and requires a new commit trace.

Phase 19 implements distributed social carrier records. Directed agent relations, role assignments, communication links, authority grants, property claims, document-backed rules, practice associations, and attested agreements retain explicit traces and opaque schemas. `OrganizationId` groups records but never receives perception, belief, intent, memory, or behavior. Rule and agreement records preserve competing interpretations and provenance without making validity or enforcement objective.

Phase 20 implements material and urban physical carriers. Inventory lots preserve material, custody, location, quantity, time, trace, and optional competing property-claim support; transfers and transformations retain typed lot ancestry, while labour remains attributable to actual agents. Parcel/building records and opaque-schema infrastructure nodes/links preserve spatial topology, capacity, condition, construction materials, and traces. Custody never proves ownership, and topology never declares itself a road, sewer, or other semantic network type.

Phase 21 adds historical orchestration carriers. Bounded stages connect explicit time intervals, numeric detail, target chunks, opaque process/parameter identity, prior-stage edges, and external traces. Validated receipts must exactly continue committed dependency traces. They coordinate causal synthesis but neither classify history nor mutate domain state.
