# Causal Carriers

Causal carriers are the mechanisms by which state from one domain affects another.

## Physical Carriers

- Position and motion
- Material contact
- Energy transfer
- Field interaction
- Structural connection

## Informational Carriers

- Physical utterances
- Written documents
- Imitated practices
- Institutional records
- Reputational transmission

## Biological Carriers

- Pathogen transmission
- Genetic inheritance
- Symbiotic transfer
- Ecological pressure

## Social Carriers

- Role assignment
- Rule enforcement
- Resource allocation
- Ritual synchronization
- Spatial organization
