# Fire

Fire is a physical hazard that affects buildings, infrastructure, and urban organization. It is not merely a random event. It propagates through causal mechanisms.

## Fire Behavior

Fire propagation depends on:

- fuel availability and type;
- building materials and construction;
- spatial layout and density;
- wind and weather;
- water access and firefighting response;
- time of day and occupancy patterns.

## Consequences

Fire may produce:

- building damage or destruction;
- casualties and displacement;
- infrastructure failure;
- economic disruption;
- changes in building codes or practices;
- social trauma and memory;
- archaeological residue.

## Fire and Society

Societies develop fire-related practices:

- prevention (material choices, spacing, regulations);
- detection (watch systems, alarms);
- response (bucket brigades, professional firefighting);
- recovery (rebuilding, insurance, aid);
- memory (commemoration, changed practices).
