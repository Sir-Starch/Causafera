# Urban Growth

Cities grow through historical processes, not procedural generation. Growth patterns reflect economic, social, geographic, and institutional factors.

## Growth Mechanisms

Urban growth may occur through:

- parcel subdivision and densification;
- peripheral expansion into adjacent territory;
- vertical construction where permitted;
- infrastructure extension enabling new development;
- institutional concentration attracting population;
- economic specialization creating district boundaries.

## Growth Constraints

Growth is limited by:

- geographic barriers (water, slope, geology);
- ownership patterns;
- regulatory restrictions;
- infrastructure capacity;
- resource availability;
- social and institutional factors.

## Historical Layers

A growing city accumulates historical layers:

- old districts with narrow streets;
- newer areas with different construction techniques;
- abandoned or repurposed structures;
- infrastructure of varying ages;
- property boundaries reflecting past divisions.
