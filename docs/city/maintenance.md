# City Maintenance

City infrastructure and buildings require continuous maintenance. Without it, systems degrade and historical knowledge is lost.

## Maintenance Systems

Plan support for:

- scheduled inspection;
- reactive repair;
- preventive upkeep;
- emergency response;
- specialist trades and guilds;
- tool and material supply chains;
- documentation of procedures;
- training and apprenticeship.

## Knowledge Loss

Maintenance knowledge is fragile. When specialists die without transmitting their skills:

- practices degrade;
- repairs become less effective;
- systems accumulate failures;
- replacement parts may no longer be producible;
- infrastructure may be abandoned or repurposed.

## Maintenance and Society

Maintenance practices create social structures:

- occupational specializations;
- guilds or trade organizations;
- knowledge transmission lineages;
- territorial service areas;
- contractual relationships with property owners.
