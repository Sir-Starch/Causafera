# Streets

Streets are routes that connect parcels and buildings. They are not merely decorative geometry. They shape movement, commerce, social interaction, and information flow.

## Street Properties

A street may have:

- physical geometry (width, surface, slope);
- construction materials and provenance;
- drainage and weathering characteristics;
- lighting;
- naming history;
- traffic patterns;
- commercial activity;
- social associations;
- maintenance state.

## Network Effects

Street networks create emergent properties:

- accessibility gradients;
- property value patterns;
- social neighborhood boundaries;
- trade route efficiency;
- information propagation speed;
- disease transmission paths;
- fire spread corridors.

## Naming

Street names are not static labels. They may:

- originate from geographic features;
- commemorate events or persons;
- reflect occupational concentrations;
- change through political or social transitions;
- develop local nicknames independent of official names;
- become obsolete while persisting in documents.
