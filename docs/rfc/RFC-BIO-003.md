# RFC-BIO-003: Biological Mana Coupling and Emergent Practitioners

**Status:** Accepted architecture; implementation deferred

**Depends on:** RFC-BIO-001, RFC-MANA-001, RFC-PERCEPT-001, RFC-PRACTICE-001, RFC-TRACE-001

## Summary

Organisms contain no intrinsic semantic mana resource and receive no authoritative magic aptitude, class, spell list, or mana-point statistic. Mana remains an external physical field. Organisms may nevertheless differ in how their morphology, tissue properties, physiology, sensory access, motor control, development, and history physically couple to that field.

Rare combinations may permit unusually strong perception, modulation, retention, or release of environmental mana. A society may call such an organism a mage, but `Mage` is not Ground Truth. Ritual repetition remains the common and historically dominant route to reliable effects; rare active biological coupling supplements rather than replaces it.

## Physical boundary

The authoritative causal path is:

```text
subjective intention
→ attention and motor/physiological control
→ physical action and carrier structure
→ external mana-field interaction
→ registered mana-to-physics coupling
→ perceived consequence
```

Mana cannot inspect intention, belief, words, spell identity, practice meaning, social role, or observer classification. Intention matters only through the physical actions and organism states it causes.

An organism is not an unexplained mana source by definition. Any transfer, retention, conversion, or release must be represented as field and carrier physics with causal provenance. If future physics permits biological work or another physical process to generate a field contribution, that conversion requires explicit inputs and accounting; it is not free regeneration. No effect may appear because a semantic request was matched to an outcome.

## Multidimensional biological coupling

There is no authoritative scalar `magic_affinity`. Contextual interaction is derived from lower-level properties, potentially including:

- morphology and spatial arrangement of tissues;
- electrical, mechanical, thermal, chemical, or other registered tissue properties;
- nervous-system timing and physiological rhythms;
- sensory transduction thresholds and noise;
- motor precision, synchronization, breathing, voice, and posture;
- tolerance of field-mediated physical feedback;
- development, injury, disease, symbiosis, aging, and current physiological load;
- inherited parameters and environmental developmental effects;
- tools, worn materials, implants, architecture, and local geometry;
- local field state and the physical history of nearby carriers.

These properties may be inherited or acquired, but their useful combination is contingent. High coupling in one carrier channel or environment does not imply universal magical capability.

## Three interaction regimes

### Passive biological coupling

Ordinary biological processes may weakly perturb or respond to the local field through their real spatial and temporal structure. This does not imply conscious control.

### Active biological coupling

A rare organism may perceive physical feedback and intentionally control actions or physiological patterns precisely enough to modulate the external field. The field still receives only physical carriers. Active coupling is neither guaranteed nor population-balanced, and the simulation must not synthesize practitioners to satisfy a desired prevalence.

### Historical and ritual coupling

Repeated actions, synchronized groups, stable tools, prepared places, architecture, and long-lived practices can produce stronger or more reproducible field configurations. This remains the primary accessible basis of magic. It may permit ordinary organisms to obtain effects that rare individuals can reach more directly or explore more efficiently.

## Environmental mana retention and acquired reservoirs

Organisms have no intrinsic `mana_pool`. They may acquire a physically retained or dynamically bound concentration of environmental mana as an emergent consequence of exposure, practice, adaptation, tissue change, symbiosis, implants, equipment, or stable field hysteresis.

Ground Truth must distinguish:

- the external or locally contained field state;
- the material or biological carrier retaining it;
- spatial containment;
- dynamic binding or hysteresis;
- social claims of ownership;
- the organism's subjective belief that the mana is its own.

Retention must have a causal inflow from the environment, bounded capacity or stability, leakage or dissipation behavior, and consequences for the surrounding field. Release, use, injury, death, carrier destruction, or movement may redistribute or destabilize it. What inhabitants call “personal mana” is therefore permitted, but it is an acquired physical field/carrier state rather than an intrinsic character resource.

Evolution may eventually produce structures that retain environmental mana efficiently. Even then, an organism inherits physical structure and developmental parameters, not a prefilled numeric mana reserve.

### Congenital retained mana

An organism may nevertheless be born with retained mana. “Congenital” describes when the physical state is present, not an exemption from causality. Valid origins may include:

- transfer from the gestational or developmental environment;
- retention from an egg, seed, parent-associated field, symbiont, or constructed incubation site;
- prenatal activity of inherited carrier structures;
- developmental formation of a stable field configuration;
- a traced physical conversion process permitted by future mana physics.

The initial reserve at birth must therefore be derived from developmental history, local field state, carrier capacity, transfer/conversion rules, and deterministic variation. Heredity may transmit the structures and parameters that make congenital retention likely; it does not directly assign a semantic reserve value. Birth, separation from a parent, movement, injury, and death must conserve or explicitly transform the associated state.

To inhabitants, this may be indistinguishable from being born with “personal mana.” That interpretation is valid subjective knowledge even though Ground Truth represents a causally formed field/carrier state.

## Emergent spells

A spell is a subjective and social model of a reproducible practice. Ground Truth stores actions, carrier states, field transitions, physical effects, memories, documents, and practice ancestry—not a semantic spell entity.

Potential carriers include voice, timing, movement, breathing, geometry, materials, instruments, multiple participants, and prepared locations. A learned procedure may contain both causally necessary elements and historically preserved mistakes.

Repetition can strengthen a learned spell through several distinct carriers:

- improved perception and motor execution;
- practice standardization and transmission selection;
- better tools or geometry;
- participant synchronization;
- persistent changes in local field and material carriers;
- acquired biological adaptation or field retention.

No generic repetition counter may directly increase spell power.

Effects are not selected from a semantic catalogue. They emerge from registered field-to-physics couplings and may affect any implemented physical or biological domain for which a causal adapter exists. “Arbitrary spells” means an open combinatorial space of physical outcomes, not wish fulfillment or violation of conservation contracts.

## Predictability, variation, and randomness

Outcome depends on the complete physical context: performer state, local field, history, geometry, materials, environment, neighboring activity, and execution variation. A socially identical spell may therefore diverge across performances.

Strict replay remains deterministic. Stochastic biological or physical variation uses named deterministic RNG streams and modifies lower-level causal variables; it must not choose a high-level spell outcome from a random table. Unpredictability for inhabitants arises from incomplete observation, nonlinear response, stochastic microvariation, and mistaken causal models.

## Emergent practitioners and institutions

The engine neither creates nor rewards mages as a category. A practitioner emerges only if a causal history supplies:

```text
useful biological or instrumental coupling
× suitable environmental field
× discovery or transmission
× successful reproduction of an effect
× survival and continued practice
```

Potentially capable organisms may never discover or reproduce an effect. Conversely, weakly coupled organisms may participate effectively through collective ritual, tools, or prepared sites. Families, magical lineages, professions, schools, and classifications are historical social constructions and may misunderstand their actual causes.

## Perception and cognition boundary

Agents never read mana Ground Truth or their authoritative coupling parameters. They receive physically accessible, noisy signals through apertures and interoception, construct subjective scenes, form hypotheses, and learn practices through observed consequences. A believed spell, talent, exhaustion limit, or personal reserve may be wrong.

## Proposal, provenance, resolution, and persistence

All organism-to-field and field-to-organism changes use phase-controlled proposal/commit transitions. Significant transfers and effects retain trace ancestry. Promotion and demotion must conserve relevant field, carrier, and biological quantities and must not invent capable individuals from an aggregate distribution. Persistence must round-trip retained field/carrier state separately from subjective ownership and magical interpretations.

## Primitive versus emergent

Primitive or derived physical state may include field components, carrier properties, tissue parameters, transfer rates, bounded retention, physiological load, actions, and trace-backed effects.

Emergent or subjective concepts include mage, spell, school, affinity, personal mana, ritual meaning, magical element, talent rank, and danger class.

## Non-goals

- no universal magic organ is mandated;
- no guarantee that humans or any population develops active coupling;
- no fixed spell catalogue or elemental taxonomy;
- no direct belief-to-mana or intention-to-mana channel;
- no replacement of ritual history with individual superpowers;
- no claim that the current scalar mana field is sufficient for final coupling physics.

## Implementation gate

Before detailed human physiology or mana-capable behavior claims M2, an ExecPlan must specify concrete fixed-point carrier variables, conservation or transfer rules, named RNG streams, sensory apertures, proposal/commit ordering, resolution behavior, persistence encoding, Explanation IR evidence, negative controls, and replay tests. The implementation must demonstrate ritual coupling and a negative-control organism before rare active biological coupling is accepted.
