# RFC-META-001: Identity and Post-Biological Pattern Persistence

**Status:** Accepted

## Summary

Phase 23 treats identity persistence as a comparison among explicit research criteria, not as an authoritative property, `Soul` object, or engine verdict that two states are the same person.

## Research state

`IdentityContinuityExperiment` identifies an opaque candidate relation and owns a bounded canonical set of `ContinuityObservation` records. An observation contains:

- an opaque evidence-channel schema;
- observation time;
- earlier and later objective state fingerprints;
- signed fixed-point strength;
- a committed causal trace.

It does not contain an `AgentId`, subjective belief, English continuity type, or truth label.

`ContinuityCriterion` is a bounded canonical set of signed weights over registered opaque evidence channels. Evaluation produces one numeric score per criterion and the supporting traces. It never reduces competing criteria to an authoritative boolean or selects a final metaphysical theory.

## Cross-world and post-biological use

Transfer receipts, biological termination records, document lineages, practice lineages, cognitive-pattern measurements, and mana-field observations may eventually supply evidence through separately registered adapters. The criterion layer does not infer personal sameness from payload correspondence, memory similarity, social recognition, causal ancestry, or any other single carrier.

The same evidence may support one criterion and oppose another. Branching, copying, partial retention, and contradictory social claims therefore remain representable without creating a hidden soul identity.

## Determinism and bounds

Observations, criteria, and channels have hard caps. Typed IDs and fixed-point integer scores determine canonical order. Evaluation uses saturating integer arithmetic and contains no RNG, locale, strings, system time, floating point, pointer identity, or hash iteration.

## Agent and observer boundary

These records are developer research state over objective traces, not agent cognition. Agents may construct subjective continuity beliefs through ordinary perception, memory, concepts, documents, and testimony. A future Explanation Engine may display criterion scores with provenance and confidence but cannot turn them into Ground Truth or feed them back into simulation.

## Decisions

- **Accepted:** bounded trace-backed observations over opaque evidence channels.
- **Accepted:** multiple explicit weighted criteria with separate numeric results.
- **Accepted:** no authoritative same-person flag and no primitive `Soul` object.
- **Deferred:** concrete biological, cognitive, social, mana, death, and transfer evidence adapters.
- **Deferred:** observer projection, persistence, population research, and final metaphysical interpretation.
