# RFC-META-001: Identity and Post-Biological Pattern Persistence

**Status:** Proposed

## Summary

Research the physical basis of identity persistence, death, reincarnation, and cross-world memory.

## Motivation

Avoid primitive `Soul` objects without an accepted model.

## Details

To be developed during Phase 23.
