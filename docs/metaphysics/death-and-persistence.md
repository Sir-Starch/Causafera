# Death and Persistence

Death is the end of biological continuity. What happens after death is a metaphysical research question, not a primitive engine feature. The project must investigate whether and how patterns associated with a living agent might persist after biological death.

## Biological Death

Biological death is a physical process:

- Cessation of vital functions
- Cellular degradation
- Decay and decomposition
- Return of materials to ecological cycles

This is Ground Truth. It is not controversial or mysterious in the simulation.

## Post-Biological Persistence

The metaphysical question is whether anything associated with the living agent persists after biological death:

- **Memories** - Do memories survive in documents, oral traditions, or other agents' minds?
- **Patterns** - Do behavioral or informational patterns persist in institutions or practices?
- **Mana structures** - Do repeated practices create mana patterns that outlive the practitioner?
- **Identity patterns** - Does the agent's identity pattern persist in some substrate?

## Ghosts as Research Target

Ghosts are a target emergent phenomenon. Candidate hypothesis:

> Persistent distributed information and mana structures may retain some pattern associated with a deceased agent, producing observable effects.

This is not a primitive `Ghost` entity. It is a research question about whether and how post-biological pattern persistence could produce phenomena that agents interpret as ghosts.

## Reincarnation as Research Target

Reincarnation is a target emergent phenomenon. Candidate hypothesis:

> Identity patterns may persist in a distributed form and rebind to new biological substrates under certain conditions.

This is not a primitive `Reincarnation` system. It is a research question about whether identity-pattern persistence and rebinding could produce phenomena that agents interpret as reincarnation.

## Societal Responses to Death

Different societies may have different beliefs about death and persistence:

- Some may believe in complete annihilation
- Some may believe in spiritual survival
- Some may believe in reincarnation
- Some may believe in ancestor persistence

These beliefs are subjective concepts. They may affect behavior, which may create physical patterns, which may couple with mana.
