# Gods and Spirits

Gods and spirits are target emergent phenomena. They are not primitive engine entities. The project must investigate whether and how persistent distributed information and mana structures could produce phenomena that agents interpret as divine or spiritual.

## The Emergent Hypothesis

Candidate hypothesis:

> Persistent distributed information and mana structures may form stable stateful attractors.

A religious system produces:

- Repeated names
- Symbols
- Synchronized practices
- Architecture
- Calendars
- Recurring behavioral structures

These patterns may create a stable mana attractor. The attractor may eventually exhibit state persistence and responsive behavior. If sufficiently agent-like, societies may classify it as a god.

## What Gods Are Not

Gods are not:

- Primitive `God` objects with `power` and `domain` fields
- Omniscient observers of simulation state
- Direct manipulators of Ground Truth
- Implementations of theological doctrines

Agents may believe in gods. The simulation does not need to implement those beliefs as primitive physical objects. However, the physical patterns produced by religious practice may create real phenomena that reinforce those beliefs.

## Spirits as Distributed Patterns

Spirits may be smaller-scale attractors:

- A spirit of a place may be a mana pattern associated with a specific location's history
- A spirit of a practice may be a mana pattern associated with a specific repeated behavior
- An ancestral spirit may be a persistent pattern associated with a deceased agent's behavioral legacy

## Religious Systems as Magical Technologies

Religious systems naturally create synchronized and persistent patterns. Therefore they may become effective magical technologies even when theology is factually incorrect.

A ritual may work not because the god exists as described but because the ritual creates a physical pattern that couples with mana. The theological explanation is a subjective causal model. The physical effect is a real pattern.

## Research Status

RFC-META-002 is accepted as a read-only numeric research architecture. It deliberately creates no god or spirit entity and makes no emergence claim. Gods and spirits remain possible agent/observer interpretations of future evidence, not Ground Truth categories.
