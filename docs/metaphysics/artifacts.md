# Artifacts

Artifacts are objects with persistent magical properties that developed through use rather than being intentionally enchanted. They are a target emergent phenomenon, not the result of a primitive `EnchantItem` action.

## The Emergent Process

Candidate process:

```text
material object
+
persistent repeated use
+
stable physical pattern
+
local mana coupling
+
historical persistence
```

An artifact is not created by a single magical act. It develops gradually through the accumulation of repeated use patterns that couple with local mana fields.

## Examples of Artifact Formation

### The Bell

A bell used at the same time for centuries:

- Creates a stable temporal recurrence pattern
- Produces a consistent acoustic signature
- Is associated with a specific location and practice
- May develop persistent mana coupling
- May eventually exhibit properties beyond a normal bell

### The Currency Token

A currency token repeatedly exchanged through millions of social transactions:

- Creates a massive repeated informational pattern
- Is associated with economic and social relationships
- May develop unusual mana properties
- May become a focus of ritual or superstitious practice

### The Tool

A craft tool used by generations of practitioners:

- Accumulates motor skill patterns in its wear marks
- Is associated with specific techniques and materials
- May develop properties that assist or resist certain uses

## What Artifacts Are Not

Artifacts are not:

- Items with a primitive `enchantment` field
- Objects created by a single spell or ritual
- Items with predefined magical effects
- Objects that respond to semantic meaning

## Artifact Properties

Candidate artifact properties:

- **Persistence** - The magical properties remain stable over time
- **Specificity** - The properties are tied to the object's history of use
- **Responsiveness** - The object may react to certain conditions or users
- **Accumulation** - Properties may strengthen with continued use
- **Degradation** - Properties may weaken if use patterns change

## Research Status

Artifact formation is a research target. Do not implement it as decided architecture.
