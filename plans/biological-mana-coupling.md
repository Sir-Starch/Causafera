# Biological Mana Coupling ExecPlan

**Status:** Accepted — blocked

## Goal

Implement RFC-BIO-003 without intrinsic mana points, semantic magic state, or direct cognition-to-mana access, while preserving ritual interaction as the common mechanism and allowing rare acquired or biological coupling to emerge.

## Context

The minimal mana field responds to physical recurrence and the biology crate provides only narrow structural contracts. Detailed biology and real domain-specific mana effects are not yet mature enough for implementation.

## Relevant invariants

INV-001 through INV-004, INV-014 through INV-019, INV-024, INV-027 through INV-035, INV-039, and INV-040.

## Ontology domains affected

Biology, mana, physics, perception, cognition, practices, heredity, development, populations, history, and society.

## Causal carriers affected

Biological structure and physiological rhythms, sensory signals, motor actions, material tools, local geometry, physical pattern history, mana-field state, and field-to-physics effects.

## Relevant documents

RFC-BIO-003, RFC-MANA-001, RFC-PERCEPT-001, RFC-PRACTICE-001, biological architecture, heredity, physiology, invariants, and primitive-versus-emergent ontology.

## Current state

The runtime has a fixed-point external field, recurrence-based response, limited feedback, and a minimal actor body. It has no detailed physiology, biological mana carrier, acquired retention, practitioner discovery, or learned spell process.

## Proposed architecture

Add concrete fixed-point biological carrier properties; physical sensory access to field consequences; proposal-only organism/field transfer; bounded acquired retention in spatial or material carriers; action-driven active modulation; ritual/practice integration; learning from subjective evidence; and conservation-safe population aggregation.

## Primitive vs emergent review

Physical carrier and field state are authoritative. Mage, spell, affinity, personal mana, school, and magical taxonomy remain subjective, social, or observer classifications.

## Non-goals

No MP statistic, spell catalogue, semantic elements, guaranteed practitioners, wish fulfillment, direct belief coupling, or implementation before detailed biological state exists.

## Implementation stages

1. Specify and validate field-to-matter/biology conservation and fixed-point variables.
2. Implement ordinary weak biological coupling and negative controls.
3. Add physically accessible perception and interoceptive feedback.
4. Connect embodied actions and practice programs to physical carriers.
5. Implement bounded environmental transfer, physical conversion where supported, retention, leakage, release, and death/destruction behavior.
6. Add heredity, gestational/developmental acquisition, congenital retention, adaptation, injury, and physiological costs without a scalar aptitude.
7. Implement rare discovery, subjective learning, transmission, and ritual-versus-active comparative experiments.
8. Add resolution, persistence, Explanation IR, and bounded long-run validation.

## Verification

Same-seed replay; reordered-batch equivalence; no-intention-access structural tests; no-field and no-coupling negative controls; environmental depletion/redistribution accounting; persistence roundtrip; resolution conservation; ritual baseline; rare active-coupling counterfactuals; and trace-complete explanations.

## Benchmark plan

Benchmark representative organism batches, field volumes, ritual participant counts, active carriers, and resolution transitions before scale claims.

## Determinism impact

All numeric authoritative state uses fixed-point rules. Variation uses named deterministic RNG streams with canonical iteration and recorded parameters.

## Memory impact

Detailed carrier state is restricted to causally relevant organisms and locations; aggregate distributions require explicit conserved moments and promotion contracts.

## Observer impact

Deferred until stable read models exist. Observer projections expose evidence and uncertainty, never authoritative `Mage` or spell classifications.

## Explanation impact

Explanation must distinguish physical coupling, retained field, execution quality, ritual history, learned belief, and social classification with trace support and confidence.

## Persistence impact

Future snapshot sections must separate field/carrier retention, biological state, practices, and subjective interpretations.

## Cross-domain effects

Ecology may produce mana-coupled organisms; society may form lineages and schools; language and writing alter physical practice carriers; tools and architecture stabilize effects; pathogens and symbionts may modify coupling.

## Risks

Hidden scalar affinity, ritual monoculture, semantic spell dispatch, non-conserved personal mana, direct Ground Truth perception, artificial practitioner quotas, and premature claims based on the scalar prototype field.
