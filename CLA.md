### Questions reserved for legal review

The following are known to be unresolved. They are recorded here rather than answered with improvised legal language, and they are inputs to the review above:

1. **Evidentiary sufficiency of electronic acceptance.** Whether an acceptance record produced by a hosted CLA service — an authenticated GitHub account, a comment or click, a timestamp, and a service-side log — is sufficient under Netherlands law to establish a binding agreement, and what retention format best supports that.
2. **Pseudonymous accepting party.** Whether identifying the Project Maintainer publicly through a GitHub identity, with legal identity disclosed only outside the repository, affects formation or enforceability of this agreement.
3. **"Irrevocable" copyright licence under Dutch law.** Whether the perpetual and irrevocable characterization in section 2 is fully effective in the Netherlands, and how it interacts with moral rights that cannot be entirely waived.
4. **Scope of the outbound-licensing grant.** Whether section 4's combination of an unrestricted outbound grant with a binding public-availability condition is enforceable as written, in particular the condition's durability against a later commercial licensee.
5. **Employment clearance.** Whether the representation in section 5.3 is adequate, or whether a separate corporate CLA is needed for contributors submitting in the course of employment.
6. **Choice of law and consumer protection.** Whether the Netherlands choice of law in section 10 holds for contributors who are consumers resident elsewhere, particularly within the EU.
7. **Attribution and licence notices.** Whether the attribution commitment in section 7 is consistent with the notice-preservation requirements of AGPL-3.0-only and the attribution requirements of CC BY-SA 4.0 in every distribution channel.
8. **Termination mechanics.** Whether section 8's written-notice mechanism needs a defined delivery channel and effective date to be operable.
